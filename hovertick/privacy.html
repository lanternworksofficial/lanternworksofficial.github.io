HoverTick Privacy Policy

Developer: Lanternworks · Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: HoverTick does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

1. Who we are

HoverTick ("the app") is a floating timer, stopwatch, interval timer and counter for Android, published on Google Play by Lanternworks ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

3. Information stored only on your device

To work, HoverTick saves the following locally on your device. This information is never sent to us or to any third party:

WhatWhy
Your timers, their names, durations, interval and counter settings, and recent timersTo run your timers and let you start them again
Saved routines (presets) and the names you give themSo you can start them in one tap
App settings (bubble size, opacity, position, sound, vibration, theme, language)To remember your preferences
Whether you have unlocked HoverTick ProSo Pro features keep working offline

Android's cloud backup is turned off for HoverTick, so this data is not uploaded to Google backups. If you use Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer when setting up a new device, the system may copy this local data to your new phone; that transfer is handled by Android and does not involve us.

4. In-app purchases

HoverTick Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy it's needed
Display over other appsTo show the floating timer bubble while you use other apps. It is only shown for timers you start.
Foreground serviceTo keep your timers running accurately while the bubble is on screen, with a visible notification.
NotificationsTo show the running-timer notification and to alert you when a timer finishes.
VibrationTo vibrate when a timer finishes, if you have vibration turned on.
Run at startupTo offer to restore your running timers after your phone restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect information about you.

6. Sharing and selling of data

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information, because we do not collect any.

7. Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing timers and routines in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling HoverTick.

8. Children's privacy

HoverTick is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and Android's app sandboxing.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email us at lanternworks.official@gmail.com.
