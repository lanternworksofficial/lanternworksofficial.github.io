A narrative card game by Lanternworks · Android · Ages 13+

Climb thirteen floors. Let the common book remember.

Oblation is a choice-driven climb through a tower that remembers every attempt. Read each card, weigh the cost and choose a path. Your meters are always close to the edge, and every decision can change who stands with you.

A tower that remembers

When a climb ends, the common book keeps the discoveries that matter. Uncover people, endings and storylines across many lives, and keep your own short notebook as you go.

Free to start

The first four floors are free. After that, choose a one-time purchase that unlocks the full game and removes all ads, or keep playing free with ads between lives. No account is required.

Oblation is being prepared for testing. The Play Store link will be added when available.
