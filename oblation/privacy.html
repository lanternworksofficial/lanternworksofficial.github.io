Privacy policy

This policy explains how Lanternworks handles information when you use Oblation, our single-player Android game (application ID com.lanternworks.oblation).

In short: Oblation has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, and release builds send pseudonymous usage, performance and crash information to Google Firebase. We never sell your information.

1. No account

Oblation does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone. There is no sign-in and no chat, and nothing you write in the game is shared with other players.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, on your device only. Lanternworks does not receive it:

  • Your current climb and the in-progress state of a life, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Collection progress: endings reached, people met, storylines discovered and the game's journal.
  • Your personal notebook entries.
  • Settings such as sound, volume, text size and reduced motion, and whether you finished the introduction.
  • Which purchases you have unlocked, so they keep working offline, and ad-pacing counters (for example how many ads were shown today, and the extra note slots you have earned).

If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this saved game in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Android backup settings; Lanternworks cannot access it.

3. Purchases

Oblation offers optional one-time purchases through Google Play Billing: one unlocks the full game, removes ads and includes unlimited climb resets; another unlocks unlimited climb resets on its own. Google handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.

4. Advertising and consent

If you play the free version beyond the first floors, Oblation shows full-screen ads between lives and may offer optional rewarded ads, using Google AdMob. No ads are shown after the full game is purchased. Depending on your choices and location, Google Mobile Ads may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic information for serving ads, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your privacy choices before ads are requested. You can review those choices later from the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Analytics, performance and crash reports

Release versions of Oblation use these Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics records pseudonymous game events such as floors reached, lives and endings, the purchase screen and your choice on it, purchases, ads shown, and optional rewarded events offered or accepted. These events contain small values such as a floor number or ad type. They do not contain your notebook or anything you write.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating-system version and an installation identifier.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring measures app start-up time, screen rendering and how long the game takes to load and resolve cards, with device and network information.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads settings, such as how often ads may appear, at most about once a day. To do this it uses an installation identifier.

We use this information to fix problems, improve performance and understand how the game is played. Development builds do not send this information.

6. Other Google Play features

The game may use Google Play's in-app update prompt to tell you when a new version is available, and may ask once, after you reach your first ending, whether you want to rate the game through Google Play's in-app review. Both are handled by Google Play.

7. Who processes information

We share information only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Processing may take place outside your country under Google's terms and safeguards.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • Google advertising privacy information

8. Retention and deletion

Data saved on your device stays until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the game. Data held by Google services is kept according to their retention settings and terms. Analytics and diagnostic data are not linked to an account, because Oblation has none. If you would like us to look into deleting data associated with your installation, contact us below.

9. Age audience

Oblation is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It contains fictional peril and death described in text. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that involved their information, contact us so we can address it.

10. Security and changes

Your local game data is protected by your device's own security, and our service providers use secure transport. No system can guarantee absolute security. If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
