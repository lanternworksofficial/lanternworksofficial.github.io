Delete your Fracture account or data

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Request by email

Email lanternworks.official@gmail.com with the subject “Fracture account deletion”. Tell us whether you want to delete your account and associated data or request removal of specific data.

  • For a linked account, contact us from the Google account's email address where possible.
  • Tell us if you used a testing build, the public app, or both.
  • For a guest account, mention that you played as a guest. If the app is still installed, the in-app action is the most direct way to identify that account. We may be unable to identify an anonymous account from a display name alone.
  • Never send a password, sign-in token or verification code. We may ask for additional non-secret information to verify ownership.

Delete in the app

Open Profile (also reachable from Settings → Account), choose Delete account or Delete my data, review the confirmation and reauthenticate if requested. A network connection is required. The app starts a fresh guest after successful deletion.

What is removed

The in-app action removes the active test/live profile (including its stored stats, streaks, Career progress and history) and Firebase Authentication account. It resets the saved local profile. It does not delete your Google account.

For a support request, we will review associated test/live profiles and multiplayer records as well as the account you identify. Lobby copies and the other environment's profile are not automatically removed by the current in-app action. Some app-level preferences, local daily share records and the home-screen widget's puzzle data remain on the device until app storage is cleared. Independent copies on other devices may also remain locally.

Retention and request handling

We verify ownership before processing requests and reply about completion or any data that must be retained for a stated legitimate reason. Advertising and crash-reporting providers may retain records under their own policies; these are not automatically erased by in-app account deletion. See the privacy policy for details and providers.
