A number puzzle by Lanternworks · Android · Ages 13+

Small moves. Surprising solutions.

Fracture is a thoughtful number puzzle where small changes reveal surprising solutions. Place operators, join digits, and reshape each equation to reach its target.

A daily moment of discovery

Build a daily streak and share a spoiler-free result. Follow a guided tutorial, restore the Fractured City across 200 Career levels, or explore Infinite Mode at your own pace with an optional stopwatch.

A little friendly competition

Create a private-code multiplayer lobby for up to four players. Choose the question count, digit range, hints and stopwatch, then compare match standings. Hosting requires a linked account; guests can join.

Make it yours

Choose distinctive themes, musical feedback and soothing background ambience. Earn permanent themes through Career milestones or use the optional 24-hour all-theme rewarded offer. Tap or drag operators, use hints, and undo a move when you want another approach.

Fracture is being prepared for closed testing. The public Play Store link will be added when available. The app includes banner ads and optional rewarded ads.
